## 2. LinkedIn — Technical Research & Engineering Preview
```text
Introducing a first look at UPI Offline Mesh — an experimental research prototype exploring resilient, offline-first digital commerce.

When cellular networks or regional power switches fail, digital transactions come to a standstill. UPI Offline Mesh investigates how peer devices can securely generate, sign, and store-and-forward payment instructions over a local mesh until a participating device regains network connectivity.

Key architectural concepts:
• Hardware-backed cryptographic signing (RSASSA-PSS SHA-256)
• Zero-knowledge relay privacy (RSA-OAEP + AES-256-GCM)
• Nonce-based replay protection & idempotent settlement
• Double-entry ledger audit and deferred reconciliation

Prototype in active development. Coming soon.

(Note: Experimental research project. Not affiliated with NPCI, UPI, or commercial banking infrastructure.)
```
