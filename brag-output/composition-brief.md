# Hyperframes Composition Brief: UPI Offline Mesh (3:4 Ratio)

## Objective
Create a cinematic, premium 23-second product teaser for UPI Offline Mesh in **3:4 aspect ratio**, matching the app's signature black and vibrant yellow (`#FFE600`) visual identity with bold **Bricolage Grotesque** typography.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Aspect Ratio: **3:4** (1080x1440 portrait)
- Duration: 23.0 seconds (690 frames at 30fps)
- Poster Frame: `brag-output/brag.jpg` (baked into Frame 0)

## Visual Identity & Design Tokens
- **Canvas / Void Black**: `#080A0D` / `#050608` with radial depth to `#16181E`
- **Surface Containers**: `#111317` with subtle golden borders `rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.25)`
- **Hero Accent (Dynamic Mesh Gold)**: `#FFE600` / `#FACC15` (matching `DesignTokens.Colors.meshGold` in the iOS app)
- **Ambient Accents**: Technical amber pulses, gold glowing drop shadows
- **Primary Typography**: `Bricolage Grotesque` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 600, 700, 800)
- **Technical Monospace**: `JetBrains Mono` for cryptographic hashes, nonces, and protocol tags

## Storyboard (3:4 Vertical Adaptation)
1. **Scene 1 (0.0s – 3.5s)**: *The Disconnection* — Signal bars drain to zero with red warning badge. "WHAT IF CONNECTIVITY DISAPPEARED?"
2. **Scene 2 (3.5s – 7.5s)**: *The Offline Ignition* — Large ₹10.00 instruction in vibrant yellow, signed locally via RSASSA-PSS SHA-256. "A DIFFERENT APPROACH TO DIGITAL PAYMENTS."
3. **Scene 3 (7.5s – 12.0s)**: *The Peer Mesh* — Stacked mesh nodes (Alice → Carol → Bridge) with dedicated vertical transit rail for the encrypted packet. "PEER-TO-PEER. MESH-POWERED. OFFLINE-FIRST."
4. **Scene 4 (12.0s – 16.0s)**: *Trust by Design* — Hardware digital signatures and hybrid RSA-OAEP + AES-256-GCM zero-knowledge envelopes. "SIGNED. ENCRYPTED. DESIGNED FOR RESILIENCE."
5. **Scene 5 (16.0s – 19.5s)**: *Deferred Reconciliation* — Atomic double-entry ledger balance sync (-₹10.00 / +₹10.00 settled). "DESIGNED TO RECONCILE WHEN CONNECTION RETURNS."
6. **Scene 6 (19.5s – 23.0s)**: *The Reveal & Vision* — Concentric radar pulse, bold Bricolage Grotesque title card, vibrant yellow tagline, and prominent "COMING SOON" badge.
